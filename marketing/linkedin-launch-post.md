I built a small tool to solve a very specific Clash of Clans problem: keeping track of every upgrade and knowing exactly which Builder becomes available next.

Meet **Village Timer** — an unofficial Home Village upgrade tracker built as a lightweight web app.

Here’s what it does:

🏗️ Imports your in-game village Data Export JSON — no Supercell login required.

⏱️ Shows active upgrade and research countdowns, sorted by the earliest completion first.

🦸 Counts Hero Hall upgrades against regular Builder availability, so a Minion Prince upgrade doesn't get missed.

🛠️ Separates extra Builder jobs, such as the Goblin Builder, from regular Builder usage.

🐾 Tracks the active Home Village building, hero, troop, spell and pet timers available in the export.

📱 Uses a mobile-friendly, Clash-inspired interface, with data saved locally in your browser.

I built it for my own village first, then kept improving it as I found edge cases in the actual game export — including Hero Hall Builder usage and the difference between regular and extra Builder slots.
